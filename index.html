About Me

Hi, I’m Eimear! I love turning simple ingredients into sweet, cozy moments.

I started baking when I was a kid. My granny used to make what I’d call old-school, traditional bakes like Bakewell tarts, and my mam would teach me all their little tips and tricks. Baking was always just a normal part of life growing up.

Now, it’s something I genuinely love and find so much comfort in. I feel like not enough people bake anymore, and there’s nothing better than homemade treats -they just taste better, and to me, they feel like a healthier, more wholesome option too. Bake With Me is my little space to share that love. I post simple, comforting recipes that anyone can try. No pressure, just enjoying the process.

A few little things about me: I’m definitely a coffee and tea addict, and my favourite desserts are cheesecake and chocolate chip cookies 🤍

Let’s bake together 🍰
