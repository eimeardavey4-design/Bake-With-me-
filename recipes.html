Beginner Sourdough

Ingredients

  • 150g active sourdough starter
  • 250g warm water
  • 25g olive oil
  • 500g bread flour
  • 10g fine sea salt

Process

  1. Mix starter, water, olive oil, flour, and salt into a rough dough.
  2. Rest the dough for 30 to 60 minutes.
  3. Cover and let it rise until puffy and nearly doubled.
  4. Shape into a round loaf.
  5. Bake in a Dutch oven for a crisp crust.

Victoria Sponge Sandwich

Ingredients

  • 225g baking spread
  • 225g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Strawberry jam
  • 300ml whipped double cream

Process

  1. Preheat oven and line two sandwich tins.
  2. Beat all sponge ingredients together until smooth.
  3. Divide into tins and bake until golden.
  4. Cool fully on a wire rack.
  5. Fill with jam and cream, then dust with sugar.

Mini Egg Brownies

Ingredients

  • 200g dark chocolate
  • 200g unsalted butter
  • 4 medium eggs
  • 275g light brown sugar or caster sugar
  • 100g plain flour
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • 150g chocolate chips
  • 250g mini eggs

Process

  1. Preheat oven and line a 9x9 tin.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter, then let it cool slightly.
  3. Whisk eggs and sugar until paler and doubled in volume.
  4. Fold in chocolate mixture, then flour and cocoa.
  5. Fold in chips and mini eggs, then bake for 25 to 30 minutes.

Basic Cupcakes

Ingredients

  • 175g self-raising flour
  • 110g caster sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 110g soft margarine or butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 50ml water or milk
  • 170g softened butter
  • 225g icing sugar

Process

  1. Preheat oven and line a cupcake tray.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Add eggs and butter, then beat until combined.
  4. Add milk or water until the batter is light and creamy.
  5. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes and finish with buttercream.

NYC Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 125g unsalted butter
  • 100g light brown sugar
  • 75g white granulated sugar
  • 1 medium egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • 300g chocolate chips

Process

  1. Cream the butter and sugars together.
  2. Beat in the egg and vanilla.
  3. Mix in flour, baking powder, bicarbonate of soda, and salt.
  4. Stir through the chocolate chips.
  5. Shape, chill, and bake until golden at the edges.
